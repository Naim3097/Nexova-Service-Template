    Questions

    Before you book.

    Anything else — message us on WhatsApp and a human will answer, usually within the hour during studio time.

    How long does a full set take?

    Classic sets take about 90 minutes; Hybrid and Wispy around two hours; a full Volume set up to two and a half. Refills are 60 to 75 minutes. You can nap — most people do.

    Why do I need to pay a deposit?

    The deposit holds a chair that would otherwise be given to someone else. It is deducted from your total, so you are not paying extra — just paying part of it earlier.

    It also means we never double-book to protect against no-shows, which keeps your appointment starting on time.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your appointment using the link in your WhatsApp confirmation. Cancellations with more than 24 hours’ notice are refunded in full to your original payment method within 3 to 5 working days.

    Same-day cancellations and no-shows forfeit the deposit.

    How should I prepare?

    Arrive with clean lashes — no mascara, eyeliner or lash serum that day. Skip caffeine if you find it hard to lie still, and remove contact lenses. If it is your first visit, come 10 minutes early for a short consultation and patch test.

    How long will my lashes last?

    Extensions shed with your natural lash cycle, so expect three to five weeks of wear with a refill every two to three weeks to keep them full. Aftercare is included with every set and makes a real difference.

    I have lashes from another studio. Can I still book?

    Yes. Book a Lash Removal first, or a Removal plus a new set on the same visit. We do not refill work from other studios, as we cannot vouch for the adhesive or isolation underneath.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    No — every booking is by appointment so that no client is ever kept waiting. Same-day slots do open up; the calendar on this page always shows what is actually free.
